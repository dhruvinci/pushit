Some behind-the-scenes footage from the music videos we shot with our friends Maneating Orchid, in the lead-up to their third album *Cold Logic*. The videos for *Binary Contagion* and *Cosmic Shroud* were directed by Josh, and the *Void Engine* visualiser was made by Kaushal.

::clip DYg5deLPj9O/00

Apart from being some of the chillest dudes to hang with, it was also the most creatively enriching exercise to work with them. Not every day that we get to work on a project where every idea is taken into consideration, and the only criteria for it sticking was if it worked for the song and the feeling the band were trying to communicate.

Thank you to the boys for trusting us to the end and giving us all the freedom to experiment with our ideas, while at the same time pushing us to the creative standard they have constantly set for themselves.
